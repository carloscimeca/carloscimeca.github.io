TAV. 01 — PROFILE

About

I'm an Environmental Engineering MSc student at the Politecnico di Torino, on a twin-track path toward Chemical Engineering and Sustainable Processes. The foundation is a Bachelor's degree from the Università degli Studi di Palermo — my work since has connected that theoretical base to real engineering problems in the ecological transition.

This portfolio collects the reports and projects behind that work: sustainable industrial technologies, waste treatment and management, renewable energy resources, pollutant dynamics, and applied geophysics, alongside a parallel track in spatial modeling — GIS, cartography, remote sensing with Sentinel-2, hydrology, hydraulics, and numerical analysis in MATLAB.

What ties it together is an interest in plant-scale optimization: taking sustainability from a design principle to something that actually runs, at scale, in industry.
