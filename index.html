About Me

I am an MSc student in Industrial Environmental Sustainability at the Politecnico di Torino, with an academic track oriented towards chemical engineering. I earned my Bachelor's Degree in Environmental Engineering from the Università degli Studi di Palermo.

I am passionate about technological solutions for ecological transition, the design of treatment and energy recovery plants (such as anaerobic digestion), territorial and climatic data analysis (GIS and reanalysis), and the development of numerical and computational models.
